Don't take our word for it
  • Go offline. Once this page has loaded, turn off Wi-Fi. Adding statements, changing categories and backups all keep working, because nothing needs the internet.
  • Watch the network. Open your browser's developer tools (F12, or ⌥⌘I on a Mac), pick the Network tab, then add a statement. Nothing is sent: the only requests are this page loading its own files.
  • Read the code. It's all public on GitHub, with no trackers or analytics. The page's security policy also tells your browser to block any connection to another site.

See where your money goes

Add your bank and card statements to see your spending by month, category and merchant, plus the subscriptions and bills that repeat. It looks back at what you spent; it isn't a budgeting app.

  1. On your bank's or card's website, open your account activity and download it as a CSV file (look for "Download" or "Export").
  2. Drop the files on this page, or click Add statement. Add as many accounts as you like.
